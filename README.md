# The Atlas of Systemic Intelligence

Welcome to **The Atlas of Systemic Intelligence**, a rigorous catalog of cross-domain leverage principles filtered for four core properties:
1. **Well-supported by evidence**
2. **Counterintuitive**
3. **Generalizable across domains**
4. **High-return for the effort of applying them**

The repository is structured around a **three-tier hierarchy** to differentiate invariant natural laws from design patterns and heuristics:
- **Tier 1: Canonical Laws and Invariants:** Absolute physical, mathematical, or structural truths that govern any system matching their formal parameters.
- **Tier 2: Mechanism Operators and Design Patterns:** Structural configurations and engineering patterns that transform resources into predictable outcomes.
- **Tier 3: Application Tactics and Heuristics:** Specific coined labels, operational techniques, and behavioral heuristics (domain-specific guidelines).

Additionally, all 52 entries implement the **From Abstraction to Implementation** methodology, answering **The Five Questions** of system design and stating explicit **Boundary Conditions** defining exactly where the principle fails.
